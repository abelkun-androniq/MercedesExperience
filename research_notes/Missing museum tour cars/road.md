# Passenger and experimental cars

## Which road car adds the most to the route?

### Takeaway

Choose the Mercedes-Benz SLS AMG Coupé Electric Drive. The current SLS story shows a 2010 car photographed outdoors and models its gullwing-door release. A separately identified electric variant would give Legend 6 a working electric drivetrain. [Current SLS stop](../../stops/sls/car.js) · [Museum guide in the exhibit](https://media.mercedes-benz.be/exclusive-insights-droomautos-uit-het-mercedes-benz-museum-op-instagram-tv/)

### Cited Findings

- Mercedes photographed museum guide Pádraic Ó Leanacháin in the electric SLS in Legend 6 in 2020. A March 2026 illustrated visit also identifies the 2013 SLS AMG Electric Drive on the museum floor. [Museum guide in Legend 6](https://media.mercedes-benz.be/exclusive-insights-droomautos-uit-het-mercedes-benz-museum-op-instagram-tv/) · [March 2026 visit](https://www.theinteriorreview.com/story/2026/3/30/where-the-automobile-began-inside-the-mercedes-benz-museum-in-stuttgart-with-photos)
- Four electric motors drive the wheels individually, permitting active distribution of both drive and braking torque. The manufacturer describes the systems and production launch in 2013. [Mercedes-Benz technical release](https://media.mercedes-benz.nl/meest-exclusieve-en-dynamische-elektrische-sportwagen-ooit-mercedes-benz-sls-amg-coupe-electric-drive-de-krachtigste-supersportwagen-ter-wereld/)

### Inferences

- Put the car in a constant-radius turn and let the visitor vary the torque split between the inside and outside wheels. Mark force at each tyre and show how the imbalance helps turn the car. Photograph the electric exhibit itself. Its four-motor layout is a distinct story from either the existing SLS hinge release or the tour’s mechanical differential locks.

### Gaps

- These are dated sightings and gallery assignments. They cannot guarantee that a particular vehicle is present every day in November 2026.

## Which research vehicle adds an entirely different power source?

### Takeaway

The 1994 NECAR 1 prototype makes hydrogen fuel-cell propulsion visible at vehicle scale. Mercedes documented the actual MB 100-based van inside its Stuttgart museum and a visitor photographed it there in a report published in February 2025. [Mercedes-Benz archive photo and vehicle history](https://media.mercedes-benz.pl/30-lat-temu-mercedes-benz-rozpocza-badania-nad-zastosowaniem-ogniw-paliwowych-w-motoryzacji/) · [On-site photos](https://motorillustrated.com/mercedes-benz-museum-in-germany-4-electrified-models-not-to-be-missed/148994/)

### Cited Findings

- The experimental power unit filled the van’s entire load compartment. The official release explicitly identifies its photograph as taken in the Mercedes-Benz Museum in Stuttgart. [Mercedes-Benz archive photograph D312958](https://media.mercedes-benz.pl/30-lat-temu-mercedes-benz-rozpocza-badania-nad-zastosowaniem-ogniw-paliwowych-w-motoryzacji/)
- The same 2025 photographer saw the 2010 B-Class fuel-cell car and NECAR 1. Use the photographs for floor evidence, and manufacturer sources for engineering detail. [February 2025 visit](https://motorillustrated.com/mercedes-benz-museum-in-germany-4-electrified-models-not-to-be-missed/148994/)

### Inferences

- Let the visitor increase motor demand and trace hydrogen and air into the stack, current to the traction motor and water out. Fade the van body to show what happened to its cargo space. Use the B-Class as an optional visual comparison of packaging, without promising a second adjacent exhibit.

### Gaps

- The accessible manufacturer source confirms the Stuttgart museum, without identifying the numbered room. Verify exact floor placement before publishing wayfinding.

## Which vehicle would make a distinctive engineering stop alongside one already present?

### Takeaway

The 1960 Mercedes-Benz 300 measuring car (Messwagen) is a one-off development laboratory. Its cable literally connects it to the subject of the existing 220 S stop. [Museum Close-Up, February 2022](https://mb-france.mercedes-benz-clubs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/02/2022Messwagentxt.pdf)

### Cited Findings

- The museum describes the 300 and the W 111 220 S connected by the cable in Legend 5. Real test engineers followed the development vehicle, keeping the bulky instruments in the following car, and generally recorded the incoming data on magnetic tape. [Mercedes-Benz Museum exhibit account](https://mb-france.mercedes-benz-clubs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/02/2022Messwagentxt.pdf)
- The route has a separate 300 and 220 S, without a Messwagen entry. [Tour route](../../stops/tour.js)

### Inferences

- A visitor could drive the front car over a bump, trace the signal through the cable, then replay the trace on the recorder in the following car. The official source explains the instrumentation principle. The illustrated sensor channel should be described as an example unless the chosen measurement is documented for the exhibit. The paired display also helps clarify the existing 220 S stop’s room.

### Gaps

- The museum’s detailed description is from 2022. It proves the exact paired gallery display on that date, without certifying today’s installation. For a tightly evidenced first phase the electric SLS is stronger. The eSprinter and eActros 600 are separate temporary alternatives in Collection 5, officially scheduled 28 June 2026–4 April 2027. [Special exhibition announcement](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/f1be3681-993f-4ac9-bc02-e2b8878ddcc4)
