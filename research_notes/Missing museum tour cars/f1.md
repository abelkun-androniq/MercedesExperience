# Formula One in Stuttgart

## Which cars have exhibit-specific evidence?

### Takeaway

The 2021 Mercedes-AMG F1 W12 E Performance is the strongest hybrid-era addition. The 2020 W11 is the other works car explicitly documented in the permanent display, with an especially good steering mechanism.

### Cited Findings

- The current museum exhibition page locates the Pit Stop station in Legend 7. The museum’s 2 December 2024 release identifies the 2021 W12 mounted vertically behind the station, separate from the apparatus visitors use to change wheels. The Mercedes archive photograph of the arrangement is D845673. [Museum station](https://www.mercedes-benz.com/en/art-and-culture/museum/exhibition/formula-one-pit-stop/) · [Museum release and caption](https://sverige.mercedes-benz-clubs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/89/2024/12/2024-12-02-PI-Boxenstopp-ENG.pdf)
- The museum explicitly identified Hamilton’s 2020 W11 in its permanent Legend 7 display on 16 December 2024. The six cars in the special presentation in the entrance hall were on view only through 6 January 2025: McLaren-Mercedes MP4-23 (2008), W05, W06, W08, W09, W10. [Museum announcement](https://sverige.mercedes-benz-clubs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/89/2024/12/2024-12-16-F1-Sonderpraesentation-ENG.pdf)
- The FIA stewards’ decision describes the W11 driver pushing or pulling the steering wheel along its column to change front-wheel toe. Turning the wheel normally steers the car. [FIA Austrian GP decision, p. 5](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/decision-document/2020%20Austrian%20Grand%20Prix%20-%20Decision%20-%20Protest%20-%20Car%2044.pdf)

### Inferences

- Design W12 around changing a wheel: pause an exploded hub, wheel and central nut at each stage and compare with the physical pit-stop rig. W11 offers a second, quite different interaction: turn vs push/pull the steering wheel with a top view of both front wheels. Avoid implying access to the internals of the exhibit or a precisely measured hydraulic layout.

### Gaps

- Permanent designation describes the W11’s curatorial status in December 2024. No same-day inventory confirms the continued display of any individual chassis. The W12’s station has the strongest continuing public visitor listing.

## Which other modern cars need qualification?

### Takeaway

The W07 has conflicting location evidence in 2026. Häkkinen’s 1998 McLaren-Mercedes MP4-13 would bridge the Mercedes engine-partner era, but floor evidence identified is older than this research date.

### Cited Findings

- Stuttgart’s 2026 press information describes Rosberg’s 2016 title winner on the banked curve. In April 2026, Zaragoza’s Mobility City specifically identified a W07 Hybrid and an original W25 in its Stuttgart-sourced exhibition, scheduled 27 March 2026–31 January 2027. The organizer described tours of that exhibition on 23 November 2026. [Stuttgart tourism](https://presse.stuttgart-tourist.de/en/automobil-und-technik/die-geschichte-des-sterns-1) · [Zaragoza vehicles](https://www.mobilitycity.es/n-oticias/mercedes-benz-lleva-a-mobility-city-en-zaragoza-su-legado-en-la-competicion-con-una-exposicion-inedita-en-espana/) · [Venue dates](https://www.mobilitycity.es/explora/actividades/mercedes-benz-motorsport-el-espiritu-de-las-flechas-de-plata-zaragoza/) · [November visit](https://www.mobilitycity.es/n-oticias/una-delegacion-de-zenith-automobiles-visita-mobility-city-para-conocer-la-exposicion-de-mercedes-benz/)
- Film from a 2 November 2025 visit identifies the 1998 MP4-13 in Legend 7 at 6:00, and older visitor photos agree. Mercedes records a 200 mm narrowing of track and overall width under the 1998 rules, substantial associated aerodynamic changes, a longer wheelbase, and both championships. [Dated visitor footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MShT3tfEHbk&t=360s) · [Dated museum photos](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:McLaren_MP4/13_in_the_Mercedes-Benz-Museum) · [Mercedes racing history](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/ddead055-42be-4bef-a636-f4be30241b02)
- Existing Stuttgart route has a W196R F1 car, W25 pre-war Grand Prix car, 500 SLC and 190E Evo II in Legend 7. The distinct 300 SLR in the route is a sports car. [Route](/Users/kunabel/workspace/MercedesExperience/stops/tour.js) · [Mercedes classification](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/e4e1f6df-227a-4b15-8baa-c281f7234b1d)

### Inferences

- Hold W07 pending dated Stuttgart confirmation. Neither source establishes chassis identity or excludes a second example. An MP4-13 is a promising later addition once the label is confirmed. Show the dimensional change overlaid on its predecessor and the low nose visible on the car.

### Gaps

- No unambiguous 2026 Stuttgart room evidence found for a current MP4-13, MP4-14 or MP4-23. The 2025 exhibition alone cannot support a permanent stop.
