# Racing and record cars

## Which sports racer fills the biggest gap?

### Takeaway
Recommend the silver 1989 Sauber-Mercedes C 9, No. 63, the Le Mans winner, for the Group C endurance and twin-turbocharging gap. Its regular room is officially documented, with more recent visitor corroboration. [Mercedes-Benz race and exhibit record](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/7c42293b-3c71-428d-af25-b562111e9844)

### Cited Findings

- Mercedes-Benz's June 2019 release identifies the winning car by room, number, drivers and engine. Legend 7, No. 63, driven by Jochen Mass, Manuel Reuter and Stanley Dickens, received the 4,973 cc M 119 HL biturbo V8. It started eleventh and won in 1989. Mercedes gives around 530 kW in the race and up to 680 kW briefly in qualifying. [Mercedes-Benz](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/7c42293b-3c71-428d-af25-b562111e9844)
- A visitor documents the C 9 on level 2 in a gallery film dated 2 November 2025 at 6:03. The Petersen's 2026 Los Angeles C 9 exhibit is credited to the Keller Collection. [Dated gallery film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MShT3tfEHbk&t=363s) [Petersen vehicle record](https://petersen.org/pages/petersen-vehicle/1989-sauber-mercedes-c-9)

### Inferences

- Show exhaust driving a turbine, its shaft driving a compressor and compressed air returning to the engine. Let visitors switch between racing and qualifying outputs, with any boost curve labelled illustrative. An exhaust turbo adds a genuinely new mechanism beside the tour's existing Roots blower. [Route](../../stops/tour.js) [Mercedes-Benz engine description](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/aed94cae-cd9b-48bc-ae84-425e5e967a91)

### Gaps

- No same-day floor check or published boost map identified.

## Which record car offers a distinct mechanism?

### Takeaway
The 1938 W 125 twelve-cylinder record car combines aerodynamics with an unexpected cooling system. This is a different car from the W 125 Grand Prix single-seater. [Mercedes-Benz room and technical account](https://media.mercedes-benz.it/mercedes-benz-w-125-inesauribile-fonte-dispirazione/)

### Cited Findings

- Mercedes-Benz places the original in Legend 7's record display. Rudolf Caracciola averaged 432.7 km/h over the flying kilometre in January 1938. A chest filled with ice and water around the radiator enabled the team to dispense with a conventional cooling-air intake. [Room and record](https://media.mercedes-benz.it/mercedes-benz-w-125-inesauribile-fonte-dispirazione/) [Cooling system](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/f9a48175-8964-490e-9909-9f35cbea8a6f)

### Inferences

- Let visitors close the nose intake, run a timed sprint and see the ice melt as it absorbs engine heat. They can then try a run long enough to exhaust the supply. This introduces a new cooling trade-off to the existing radiator and drag demonstrations. [Mercedes-Benz](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/f9a48175-8964-490e-9909-9f35cbea8a6f)

### Gaps

- The official original-room attribution is from 2018. The sources disagree on ice/water quantity. Use the principle without specifying litres. [2016 Mercedes-Benz press kit](https://automobiel.mercedes-benz-clubs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/2016/01/20160617-Goodwood_Festival_of_Speed_2016_ENG.pdf) [2019 Mercedes-Benz account](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/f9a48175-8964-490e-9909-9f35cbea8a6f)

## Which other identifiable permanent exhibit is worth considering?

### Takeaway
The 1939 three-axle T 80 is a secondary choice if the tour wants a spectacular unfinished engineering project. Mercedes explicitly identifies the original body assembly as a permanent exhibit and maintains a dedicated museum page. [Museum exhibit](https://www.mercedes-benz.com/en/art-and-culture/museum/exhibition/t-80-record-giant-from-1939/) [Mercedes-Benz history](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/f9a48175-8964-490e-9909-9f35cbea8a6f)

### Cited Findings

- The record attempt never ran. The museum exhibit includes the body, grid subframe and wheels. The original chassis was prepared separately with a cutaway DB 603 engine. [Mercedes-Benz](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/f9a48175-8964-490e-9909-9f35cbea8a6f)

### Inferences

- Use the silhouette and the planned powerplant to model how required power rises with speed, explicitly marking all top speeds as targets or estimates. This overlaps with existing aero stops, so rank the C 9 and W 125 record car first. [Museum](https://www.mercedes-benz.com/en/art-and-culture/museum/exhibition/t-80-record-giant-from-1939/)

### Gaps

- No measured performance from a record run exists. No current same-day availability feed identified. [Mercedes-Benz](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/f9a48175-8964-490e-9909-9f35cbea8a6f)
