# Everyday cars, research vehicles and cultural history

## Which additions broaden the route?

### Takeaway

The 300 TD estate gives families a familiar engineering problem. The F 400 Carving introduces actively controlled tyre camber and opens the level 0 design display. Adenauer’s actual last official car has strong 2026 documentation.

### Cited Findings

- **300 TD estate (S 123), Collection 1.** A museum-guide photo explicitly places the car in Gallery of Voyagers, in a series about permanent exhibits. The original manufacturer caption is reproduced in the linked German release. Mercedes identifies automatic hydropneumatic levelling as standard on the 123 estate. Add holiday luggage and restore the rear ride height. The existing red 300 demonstrates a motor winding a torsion bar, a different means of achieving that result. A visitor feature published March 2026 describes a 300 T estate in the same gallery. [Museum guide and caption](https://automuseum-stuttgart.de/exklusive-einblicke-traumwagen-des-mercedes-benz-museums-auf-instagram-tv/) · [Mercedes engineering history](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/80c034f7-82c7-4232-8530-f6176b63405c) · [Visitor account](https://www.theinteriorreview.com/story/2026/3/30/where-the-automobile-began-inside-the-mercedes-benz-museum-in-stuttgart-with-photos)
- **2001 F 400 Carving, Fascination of Technology, level 0.** Mercedes documented this display in 2021 and a visitor subsequently described seeing it in a feature published in March 2026. Turn into a corner, tilt the two outer wheels by up to 20 degrees and show which portion of the specially designed tread meets the road. [Mercedes-authored exhibit description](https://www.thespeedjournal.com/mercedes-benz-f-400-research-vehicle/) · [2001 engineering release](https://media.mercedes-benz.be/fr/salon-de-lautomobile-de-tokyo-2001-f-400-c/) · [Visitor account](https://www.theinteriorreview.com/story/2026/3/30/where-the-automobile-began-inside-the-mercedes-benz-museum-in-stuttgart-with-photos)
- **1959 Mercedes-Benz 300 d (W 189), Collection 4.** Adenauer bought this black official car for private use when his time as chancellor ended in 1963. Visitors can find his private plates and the pennant holder. For the mechanical story, compare its manifold fuel injection to the carburettor of the existing red 1952 W 186 in Legend 4. April 2026 Mercedes coverage explicitly distinguishes both exhibits and galleries. [Identification and technical changes](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/38d17e81-c197-47df-b263-341e3e05fd8d) · [April 2026 exhibit check](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/article/08ac504a-357c-470d-bdb1-6d1f6dafcb48)
- **2001–03 C 32 AMG Estate FIA Medical Car, Collection 3.** Mercedes identified this original rescue car and its location on 2 July 2026. Four crew seats, a modular medical supply container, reinforced tyres and a supercharged V6 turn an estate into a first responder. Follow the crew through the opening lap and then show how each reaches equipment. [Museum profile](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/97ca3aa0-c724-423a-9b76-91c8e07e6a5d)
- **1983 190 E 2.3-16 Nardò record saloon, Legend 7 vertical wall.** Museum identifies the unrestored green-team car at fourth position on the wall in August 2023. Its distributor arm broke during the record attempt, but the car finished. Show a rotor routing ignition to each cylinder, then freeze it to reveal why that one component mattered. The fastest of the three cars averaged 247.9 km/h over 50,000 km; the exhibit finished third. Its story differs from the 190 E suspension and the Evo II touring-car stops. [Museum exhibit and contemporary event record](https://mb-france.mercedes-benz-clubs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2023/08/202308-40ans190_ENG.pdf)

### Inferences

- Priority order within this group is 300 TD, F 400, then the 300 d. The estate and active-camber prototype give the widest range of new experiences.

### Gaps

- The exact year of the displayed estate has conflicting visitor captions. Use S 123 and 300 TD until the label settles the year. The 2026 feature’s publication date does not necessarily establish the date of the visit.

## Which research cars have less complete documentation?

### Takeaway

Auto 2000 and F 700 both offer novel interactions. Placement relies more heavily on eyewitness material than the primary candidates above.

### Cited Findings

- **1981 Auto 2000, Legend 6.** The 2011 room-opening coverage identified the displayed powertrain as a V6 turbodiesel, supported by a visitor photograph of 2018. A visitor film published February 2026 includes it in the level 3 chapter. Mercedes confirms three test drivetrains: V8 cylinder deactivation, V6 diesel with two-stage turbocharging, and an experimental gas turbine. Swap labelled powertrains on a common body; distinguish the exhibit’s diesel from the other experiments. [Room-opening coverage](https://www.auto-medienportal.net/artikel/detail/7922) · [Visitor photograph](https://www.flickr.com/photos/93207294%40N04/44139371645/) · [Visitor film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MZZqznVzhGk) · [Contemporary manufacturer report](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/documents/investors/berichte/geschaeftsberichte/daimler-benz/daimler-ir-annualreport-1981.pdf)
- **2007 F 700** was described alongside the F 400 in a museum visit published in March 2026. Give the user a bump on the road and compare the suspension reacting on contact with the prototype’s two lasers reading the road ahead. [Visitor report](https://www.theinteriorreview.com/story/2026/3/30/where-the-automobile-began-inside-the-mercedes-benz-museum-in-stuttgart-with-photos) · [Manufacturer description of PRE-SCAN](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/documents/investors/reports/annual-report/daimler/daimler-ir-annualreport-2007.pdf)

### Inferences

- F 400 and F 700 can complement one another, with one car manipulating the contact patch while the other anticipates a disturbance.

### Gaps

- The 240 D taxi appeared in historical Collection 5 coverage. Current Collection 5 is booked by a commercial vehicle special show, and a current permanent taxi display needs fresh evidence. The C 111-III chassis list includes different physical examples, including a display car with a dummy engine. No recent museum-floor identification of a specific C 111-III was found here.
