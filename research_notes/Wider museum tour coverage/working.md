# Working vehicles and public services

## Which new working vehicles deserve a stop?

### Takeaway
Five distinct additions have operator-documented Stuttgart exhibits. Each is absent from the [route](../../stops/tour.js). The hands-on experiences below are design proposals.

### Cited findings and proposed interactions

- **1952 LF 3500 with DL 22 turntable ladder, Collection 3.** Rotate the turntable toward a building, raise the ladder and extend it to a window. Let visitors see how elevation trades horizontal reach for height. The operator identifies it alongside the separate 1912 Benz motor fire engine. Footage dated 2 November 2025 shows the LF 3500 at 8:10. [Daimler Truck exhibit record](https://www.daimlertruck.com/en/newsroom/pressrelease/which-daimler-engine-is-as-strong-as-32-firefighters-51931120) · [Dated floor film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wKrLSVv8xJg&t=490s)
- **1937 Mercedes-Benz 320 ambulance, Collection 3.** Slide the lower stretcher on its roller carriage in a rail, then explore how the second stretcher fits above it in Hohner's factory ambulance. This connects an ordinary-looking saloon nose to the unusual engineering at the back. [Museum close-up](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/article/70ee1691-585c-4066-9ef3-787ad5149158) · [English museum press release](https://sverige.mercedes-benz-clubs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/89/2024/02/20230203_Close-up_02_ambulance_ENG.pdf)
- **1974 LP 1513 heating-oil tanker, Collection 2.** The museum calls it a permanent exhibit. Ask visitors to find the coolant, a headlamp bulb and the engine oil. Open the centre grille flap, one of the side panels, and finally the service hatch between the seats. The exercise reveals how mechanics reached the engine in a fixed cab. [Daimler Truck exhibit and photographs](https://www.daimlertruck.com/en/newsroom/pressrelease/mercedes-benz-lp-1513-advent-calendar-what-is-hidden-behind-the-little-doors-51847943)
- **1938 O 10000 Austrian mobile post office, Collection 2.** Unfold the illuminated writing desk and slide a clerk's seat forward into the footwell to bring clerk and customer to eye level. Or follow a letter from hatch to sorting shelf and bag. Point out the three telephone booths along the side. [Daimler Truck exhibit and detailed photos](https://www.daimlertruck.com/en/newsroom/pressrelease/this-mercedes-benz-bus-was-in-service-with-the-austrian-post-until-the-1970s-52248815)
- **1892 Daimler motor fire pump, Legend 1.** Horses moved the carriage while its two-cylinder engine pumped 300 litres of water a minute, work Daimler equates to 32 firefighters. Switch between transport and pumping, then time how quickly the pump fills a tank. [Museum close-up](https://www.daimlertruck.com/en/newsroom/pressrelease/which-daimler-engine-is-as-strong-as-32-firefighters-51931120)
- **300 TD (S 123) estate, Collection 1.** Mercedes identifies the estate in the Gallery of Voyagers and confirms automatic hydropneumatic level control as standard on this estate family. Load the luggage bay, observe rear sag, and let fluid pressure restore ride height. Rank below the preceding choices because ride levelling already features in the tour. [Mercedes-Benz gallery identification](https://media.mercedes-benz.it/vivi-il-museo-mercedes-benz-da-casa/) · [Mercedes-Benz engineering history](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/80c034f7-82c7-4232-8530-f6176b63405c)

The sources document gallery placement, typically 2021–23, rather than a live roll call. Museum room pages identify the collections. ## Which other mechanisms and vehicles warrant mention?

### Takeaway
The snow blower is a particularly good improvement to an existing stop. The refuse vehicle has dated on-floor evidence.

### Cited findings and proposed interactions

- The existing **2004 Unimog U 500** covers portal axles. Add a step engaging its snow cutter, tracing snow through to the discharge chute and adjusting the direction of throw. Daimler gives the mechanism and museum photos. [Existing stop](../../stops/unimog/car.js) · [Daimler Truck Collection 3 record](https://www.daimlertruck.com/en/newsroom/pressrelease/the-universal-solution-against-snow-and-icy-roads-the-unimog-u-500-with-winter-maintenance-equipment-51950816)
- **2005 Econic 2628 NGT, FAUN Rotopress**, Collection 3. Dated gallery film identifies it at 2:12. Animate screw channels in the rotating drum carrying waste forwards and compacting it. [2 November 2025 gallery film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wKrLSVv8xJg&t=132s) · [FAUN cutaway and operating principle](https://faun.com/fileadmin/pdf-englisch/Prospekte/fuo_prospekt_ROTOPRESS_en_low.pdf)

### Gaps
For the Econic I found dated first-person gallery footage and manufacturer documentation on its body type, but no current operator item record spelling out the variant.

## Which temporary vehicle has recent evidence?

### Takeaway
The **1922 Benz 1C** has unusually fresh confirmation dated 24 November 2026, with a firm end date.

### Cited findings and proposed interactions

- A rotating cutaway of its Cardan shaft can show how torque reaches the rear axle as the suspension moves. Visitors can then follow the chassis through its three bodies, first a Stuttgart fire brigade crew carrier, then a tow truck, now restored as a flatbed. It is in the Collection 5 special exhibition through 4 April 2027. [Museum close-up, 24 November 2026](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/article/2f1adf4a-87d3-4eaa-af21-5ce944a3f623) · [Special exhibition](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/f1be3681-993f-4ac9-bc02-e2b8878ddcc4)

### Gaps
Placement after the exhibition ends is unknown.
