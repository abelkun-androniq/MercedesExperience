# Additional passenger cars and working mechanisms

## Which everyday cars fill gaps?

### Takeaway

The Mercedes-Benz 300 TD family estate in Collection 1 and the W 123 230 E saloon aboard the existing transporter in Collection 2 bring the tour closer to the cars a family could actually own. [Museum guide with the 300 TD](https://media.mercedes-benz.it/vivi-il-museo-mercedes-benz-da-casa/) · [Museum account of the transporter cargo](https://www.daimlertruck.com/en/newsroom/pressrelease/a-giant-aloft-the-car-transporter-at-the-mercedes-benz-museum-52709588)

### Cited Findings

- **Mercedes-Benz 300 TD estate, S 123, Collection 1.** The manufacturer explicitly locates the 300 TD in the permanent Gallery of Voyagers in its 2021 museum guide photograph. A March 2026 visitor independently identifies the estate alongside the Marco Polo. The 123 estate had automatic hydropneumatic level control as standard. [Museum guide caption, 2021](https://media.mercedes-benz.it/vivi-il-museo-mercedes-benz-da-casa/) · [2026 visit](https://www.theinteriorreview.com/story/2026/3/30/where-the-automobile-began-inside-the-mercedes-benz-museum-in-stuttgart-with-photos) · [Manufacturer's 123-series history](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/80c034f7-82c7-4232-8530-f6176b63405c)
- **Mercedes-Benz 230 E, W 123, Collection 2.** The actual final W 123 saloon off the line is on the existing 1624 car transporter. The 2024 exhibit article distinguishes it from the final 280 CE coupé, which shares the load. Bosch lists the W 123 230 E as a K-Jetronic application. Airflow deflects a sensor plate, and its lever moves the control plunger in the fuel distributor, varying continuous injection into the intake ports. [Museum exhibit article](https://www.daimlertruck.com/en/newsroom/pressrelease/a-giant-aloft-the-car-transporter-at-the-mercedes-benz-museum-52709588) · [Bosch model application](https://www.bosch-repair-service.com/en/engine-control-unit/bosch/k-jetronic-0280800040001/) · [Bosch system manual](https://w123.no/mercedes_docs/gasoline_fuel_injection_system_k-jetronic_eng.pdf)

### Inferences

- **300 TD interaction:** put suitcases in the boot. Watch the rear settle, the height valve admit hydraulic fluid and the body return to its original height under the same load. This adds hydraulic self-levelling in an everyday estate to the tour's air and torsion-bar levelling examples.
- **230 E interaction:** open the throttle and watch the airflow plate move the plunger. Track fuel delivery to all four inlet ports. Link directly from the 1624 stop so the visitor can identify which car to look for.

### Gaps

- Confirm the 300 TD exhibit's model year from its label. Do not infer a turbocharger from “TD” in the estate name. The public press source confirms the 230 E on the transporter in 2024 and does not establish its exact deck position today.

## Which experimental cars make the best interactive stops?

### Takeaway

At the end of the route there is a cluster of research cars the current app largely misses. An on-site account published in March 2026 explicitly names the F 200, F 400 and F 700 together. The museum describes the research vehicles as the transition to Fascination of Technology on level 0. [March 2026 museum visit](https://www.theinteriorreview.com/story/2026/3/30/where-the-automobile-began-inside-the-mercedes-benz-museum-in-stuttgart-with-photos) · [Museum press kit, p. 3](https://www.mercedes-benz.com/content/dam/brandhub/assets/art-and-culture/mercedes-benz-museum/museum/Media-Information-Mercedes-Benz-Museum-EN.pdf)

### Cited Findings

- **F 400 Carving (2001).** The research car can tilt the outside wheels up to 20 degrees when cornering, matching the special tyres to the task. [Mercedes-Benz technical presentation](https://media.mercedes-benz.be/autosalon-van-tokyo-2001-mercedes-benz-f400/) · [March 2026 museum observation](https://www.theinteriorreview.com/story/2026/3/30/where-the-automobile-began-inside-the-mercedes-benz-museum-in-stuttgart-with-photos)
- **F 200 Imagination (1996).** Driver inputs came from sidesticks in the door and centre console. Electrical signals took the place of a steering column and pedals. [Daimler-Benz 1996 annual report, p. 18](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/documents/investors/berichte/geschaeftsberichte/daimler-benz/daimler-ir-annualreport-1996.pdf) · [March 2026 museum observation](https://www.theinteriorreview.com/story/2026/3/30/where-the-automobile-began-inside-the-mercedes-benz-museum-in-stuttgart-with-photos)
- **F 700 (2007).** The PRE-SCAN suspension used two laser scanners in the headlights to read the road ahead of the wheels, letting each corner of the suspension adapt before reaching a bump. [Daimler 2007 annual report](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/documents/investors/reports/annual-report/daimler/daimler-ir-annualreport-2007.pdf) · [March 2026 museum observation](https://www.theinteriorreview.com/story/2026/3/30/where-the-automobile-began-inside-the-mercedes-benz-museum-in-stuttgart-with-photos)

### Inferences

- **F 400:** turn the steering and zoom in on the outside tyre. Highlight which part of the specially shaped tread touches the road as the wheel leans. Avoid assigning a grip gain to a particular road surface without tyre data.
- **F 200:** give the visitor a sidestick mapped to steering, acceleration and braking. Trace input to actuator under the body. This fits beside the tour's mechanical steering linkages.
- **F 700:** place a bump, compare the same car with and without road preview and plot body movement. It can build on the existing quarter-car illustration. Model comparative behaviour and label any assumed actuator or road figures.

### Gaps

- The dated visit supplies named-car floor evidence and the press kit establishes the research-gallery context. Neither supplies a November live vehicle roster.

## Which early cars and lower-confidence leads deserve more attention?

### Takeaway

The **1908 Mercedes 75 PS Double Phaeton** has much better floor evidence than the suggested Mercedes-Knight, 190 E Elektro or Mercédès Mixte. It is an early opportunity to show exactly what extra cylinders do at the same engine speed. [Dated visitor photograph from May 2025](https://www.flickr.com/photos/131666754%40N04/54718683169) · [Legend 2 visitor photographs](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Mercedes_75hp_Double_Phaeton_in_the_Mercedes-Benz-Museum)

### Cited Findings

- Mercedes-Benz Heritage identifies the 75 hp as Daimler-Motoren-Gesellschaft's first six-cylinder production car. Its 2024 history concerns another body style, the Spider. The Stuttgart photographs identify the museum car itself as a Double Phaeton. [Factory history of the 75 hp model](https://sverige.mercedes-benz-clubs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/89/2024/05/PI_Concours-on-Savile-Row-PI-EN.pdf) · [1908 museum vehicle photographed May 2025](https://www.flickr.com/photos/131666754%40N04/54718683169)
- A firsthand German Aerospace Center account documents the W 115 240 D taxi in Stuttgart in 2024. Older material places it in Collection 5, a space now devoted to temporary exhibits. No November 2026 placement was established, making it a follow-up lead. [DLR museum visit](https://www.dlr.de/en/media/publications/magazines/all-digital-magazines/dlrmagazine-174/a-place-amongst-the-stars) · [Current Collection 5 exhibition](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/f1be3681-993f-4ac9-bc02-e2b8878ddcc4)
- The Vision Mercedes-Maybach 6 is listed among the exhibits of the 18 October 2026 festival in Stuttgart. This updates the repository note about never having been shown and supports only a dated appearance. [Festival programme](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/abec62d6-750d-4164-94b0-bc6f58e36454)

### Inferences

- **75 PS interaction:** turn the crank through two revolutions and compare a representative evenly firing four with the six. At the same rpm, successive power strokes arrive at 180 rather than 120 crank degrees in the four. Highlight each cylinder and mark the intervals. Can reuse the tour’s engine model. Avoid asserting a particular firing order without a source.

### Gaps

- No adequate location evidence found in this pass for the Knight sleeve-valve car, Mercédès Mixte, a 190 E Elektro or a separate Benz Ideal. Do not infer a public stop from museum ownership or vehicle history. Early motorised propulsion and small-car adoption are already represented by the existing Velo.
