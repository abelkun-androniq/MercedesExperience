# Corner and wing evidence

## Is the grid check meaningful?

### Takeaway

Yes for the sign of a stated observable in this particular modeling sequence when both paired, stationary contrasts and a domain check support it. Two meshes give no numerical convergence order, validated physical Reynolds number or continuum error bound. Keep the nominal molecular Reynolds number fixed.

### Cited findings

- The corner solver fixes 680 mm as the reference length, lattice inlet speed 0.08 and molecular Re 10,000, so molecular ν in lattice units scales as 1/h and τ₀ = 0.50068 / 0.50102. Its actual BGK relaxation depends on the local non-equilibrium stress. It approaches the molecular value in uniform flow even on these coarse meshes. Smagorinsky ν_t scales with (C_s Δ)² times the strain magnitude. A small molecular τ−½ alone cannot establish that essentially *all* dissipation is from the closure. Numerical and wall dissipation remain separate. [Corner parameters](/Users/kunabel/workspace/MercedesExperience/tools/cfd/part.mjs:159) [Local relaxation](/Users/kunabel/workspace/MercedesExperience/tools/cfd/lbm3d.mjs:89)
- Explicit-filter refinement with the same physical filter on successive numerical meshes can examine discretization of a common filtered model. Grid-tied LES couples resolution/model and discretization, often non-monotonically. [Bose, Moin and You, original Stanford report](https://web.stanford.edu/group/ctr/ResBriefs08/13_bose.pdf) [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3485774)
- W 196 tyre nominal width 150 mm = 6.25 / 9.38 cells. The 48 mm gap (7.1% of diameter) is implemented by cutting the bottom of the cylinder, rather than translating the hub upward, yielding 2 / 3 clearance cells. Evo width actually 270 mm and diameter 666 mm, whereas reference Re uses 680 mm for both. [Corner geometry](/Users/kunabel/workspace/MercedesExperience/tools/cfd/corner.mjs:19)

### Inferences

- For budgeted robustness, hold physical geometry, contact/gap ratio, rotation ratio, road speed, inlet, outlet, blockage and mirrors, molecular Re, comparable low Mach, probe positions/physical scales and reduced averaging duration fixed. Keep same model and C_s=0.16 for standard h-filtered LES. Do not tune a global effective Re, τ or C_s across meshes to force agreement. If the question is the numerical error of one filtered PDE, decouple numerical h from an explicit physical filter and consistent SGS mixing length on both grids. Changing C_s alone cannot align the two numerical filters. This is a separate experiment that is usually a poor first purchase here.
- Sample instantaneous τ_eff/ν_t/ν near the wheel, gap and separated shear layer and, ideally, resolved versus modeled dissipation. Look at maximum local Mach and density swings, and voxelized solid masks for fit/comparable physical clearances. If unable to get a robust result, fix resolution/model or choose a lower stated molecular Re and recompute both states on both meshes as a different model.

### Gaps

- No saved eddy-viscosity budget, explicit filter study, rotating-wheel check, model-validation measurement or sampling error for local wing probe; no evidence for a physical drag or axle-load magnitude.

## What does the W 196 width show?

### Takeaway

No present two-grid sign. A coarse-grid descriptive observation is supported: at the chosen station the exposed version has a wider region of slow mean flow. The strip on the enclosed car sits against its flank; the exposed strip trails the tyre. They are distinct features of two body packages.

### Inferences

- Blockage need not cancel between bodies. Run **both** coarse variants at enlarged width/height and adequate inlet/outlet distance; use paired geometry/gap/rotation sensitivity to extend claims beyond the fixed lifted-tyre model. The gap admits spurious throughflow, particularly exposed. The half-car mirror excludes asymmetric global modes. Physical rolling requires appropriate moving-tread BC and a credible contact representation; a spinning staircase alone is a poor validation.
- For the existing images, describe two separate features and show the common speed scale, plane and body outline. To compare a local wake, average streamwise velocity deficit or integrate [1−⟨u_stream⟩/U_ref]_+ over a predeclared multiple-cell transverse patch that is fluid for both, with common U_ref, normalization and mask. First verify that the proposed patch contains the scientific feature in both. Where it does not, move downstream behind *both* bodies in a finite-body run, or choose the question of wheel shielding and accumulate streamwise momentum exchange specifically on the labeled tyre, using common tyre reference area. A local deficit integral is a local observable and does not yield vehicle drag (pressure, shear, and other fluxes matter). Avoid a fluid patch carved differently in the two cases, which would manufacture a contrast. Threshold, line, grid, wall-geometry and domain checks test distinct effects.
- The current public copy's “churned air,” inferred straightaway benefit, hard 23/4 cm, and depiction of the tyre turning exceed what this test measures. Historical statements may have independent sources. [W 196 copy](/Users/kunabel/workspace/MercedesExperience/stops/w196r/car.js:33)

### Gaps

- The threshold/station postprocessing used complete-field means only. No alternate window-level fields, rotating or ground-contact runs, fine exposed, or paired enlarged-domain result was available at inspection. A new common-patch or tyre-force metric merits its own paired evidence.

## Which arch and wing lessons survive?

### Takeaway

Arch yes as a picture of one model's mean flow; no comparative flare effect or two-grid claim yet. Wing yes as an archived, local, two-grid upward change in mean direction; no validated force, angle magnitude, or proven stationary sign.

### Cited findings

- The arch seed is (1500,780,344) mm, aligned with the outer part of the tyre, and its station is 600 mm behind the axle. The code zeroes the vertical velocity component when integrating this line at fixed hub-height plane. The result is a streamline of the horizontal projection of the mean velocity. It is neither a 3-D mean streamline nor a time-resolved particle trajectory. Its lateral shifts in the 24 mm window fields are +367/+374/+377 mm; the full-mean displayed path is +372 mm. There is no second grid in this corner record. ### Inferences

- Museum wording can say “In this slice the line bends out around the front corner.” Do not attribute the shift solely to the arch, conclude drag, or call it a tracked parcel. Confirm the projected line reached the station on each mesh and for neighboring seeds. Introduce a documented, geometrically resolved wheelhouse cover only if teaching the separate covered-vs-open experiment; it changes venting and exposure, and cannot identify the effect of the historical flare.
- Wing wording can say “In both recorded runs [grid pairs], raising the flap turned the mean flow farther upward in the marked box.” Keep the box fixed and report neither force nor a repeatable size. Extending the *same observable's* time record and evaluating nearby boxes / masks costs less and tests a more relevant vulnerability than an isolated wing. A nested, refined solve can be worthwhile for actual flap geometry / local flow structure after improving mesh and inflow. Sample identical upstream boundary conditions from a parent sufficiently upstream of flap influence, preserve boundary layer, downwash and unsteadiness (time history or credible fluctuations), match interfaces mass flux/pressure, use compatible side/outflow and ample overlap; validate base run against parent. A steady imposed mean misses turbulence and one-way nesting misses feedback, so it cannot buy full-scale truth. Public Evo answer currently quotes 34 kg and 9 kW, which the direction probe cannot validate. [Current Evo copy](/Users/kunabel/workspace/MercedesExperience/stops/190e-evo2/car.js:36)

### Gaps

- No fine *corner* arch or local wing-angle batches at inspection. Even a passing numerical test cannot certify the historical car's behavior from assumed voxel geometry, low Re and missing rolling wheels.
