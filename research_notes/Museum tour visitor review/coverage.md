# Museum coverage

## Which galleries does the route represent?

### Takeaway

There are 46 stops across 11 named galleries. All seven Legend galleries and Collections 1–4 have stops. Collection 5 hosts changing exhibitions, and the official tour includes a Level 0 technology area beyond the racing finale. [Route](../../stops/tour.js) · [Museum exhibition description, pp. 3–4](https://www.mercedes-benz.com/content/dam/brandhub/assets/art-and-culture/mercedes-benz-museum/museum/Media-Information-Mercedes-Benz-Museum-EN.pdf)

### Cited Findings

- Legend 1–7 stop counts are 8, 3, 4, 4, 6, 4, 4. Collection 1–4 counts are 3, 4, 2, 4. The museum explicitly places the Uhlenhaut Coupé in Legend 4, matching a strong tour stop. [Route](../../stops/tour.js) · [Museum guide](https://www.mercedes-benz.com/content/dam/brandhub/assets/art-and-culture/mercedes-benz-museum/museum/Media-Information-Mercedes-Benz-Museum-EN.pdf)
- The four Legend 6 entries teach suspension and combustion, a folding roof, a crash structure and escape hinges. The room itself tells the transition to emission-free mobility. [Route](../../stops/tour.js) · [Official Legend 6](https://www.mercedes-benz.com/en/art-and-culture/museum/exhibition/legend-6/)

### Inferences

- Choose one verified electrification exhibit for Legend 6. Let the visitor follow energy through the powertrain at launch and under braking if that exhibit supports regeneration. Add a dated Collection 5 signpost and an optional Level 0 exit prompt. These cover more of the physical visit without promising a permanent rotating exhibit.

### Gaps

- No current object-by-object inventory or floor inspection. These counts measure guide coverage only.

## Which locations warrant checking first?

### Takeaway

Two published descriptions conflict with walking directions, including one of the three teaser stops.

### Cited Findings

- The route places Blitzen-Benz in Legend 2 without a warning. Stuttgart’s tourism office places it on the banked racing curve, which the museum identifies as Legend 7. [Route](../../stops/tour.js) · [Stuttgart tourism press office](https://presse.stuttgart-tourist.de/en/automobil-und-technik/die-geschichte-des-sterns-1) · [Museum guide](https://www.mercedes-benz.com/content/dam/brandhub/assets/art-and-culture/mercedes-benz-museum/museum/Media-Information-Mercedes-Benz-Museum-EN.pdf)
- The O 302 stop already flags room uncertainty but sorts into Collection 1. Dated November 2022 photos explicitly place this World Cup recreation in Collection 4. The manufacturer’s photographs show the fan by the front door and the Tip and Tap mascots on the rear, two useful physical recognition cues. [Route](../../stops/tour.js) · [Daimler Truck photo captions](https://www.daimlertruck.com/en/newsroom/pressrelease/maximum-bus-comfort-for-the-1974-world-cup-footballers-52102483)

### Inferences

- Prioritize a dated floor or curator check for these two stops. Publish the last documented gallery and date alongside any unresolved current location. Check route index, stop header and teaser order after correction.

### Gaps

- A dated press photo establishes past placement. Current floor placement and exhibit loans remain unverified.
