# Which cars should join the museum tour?

**Add the 2021 Mercedes-AMG F1 W12 first, the SLS AMG Coupé Electric Drive second and the 1989 Sauber-Mercedes C 9 third.** Together they give visitors three new things to try: change an F1 wheel, steer a car with motor torque and put exhaust gas to work. The tour already has the 1950s W 196 R Formula 1 car. Its W 25 belongs to the pre-war Grand Prix era, and its 300 SLR is a sports racer. The gap is modern F1, alongside electric propulsion and Group C endurance racing. As of 26 November 2026, public sources establish gallery placement with different levels of confidence. The W12 has the strongest continuing visitor-facing exhibit record. ## Hamilton’s W11 would make the best second F1 stop

The **2020 W11** offers the best F1 mechanism to animate. Turn the steering wheel and the front wheels steer together. Push or pull it along its column and their **toe** changes: seen from above, their leading edges move closer together or farther apart. A top view with those two independent controls would explain what made dual-axis steering unusual. Mercedes explicitly placed Hamilton’s W11 in the **permanent Legend 7 exhibition in December 2024**. Confirm its current position before adding it to the walking route. [Museum statement](https://sverige.mercedes-benz-clubs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/89/2024/12/2024-12-16-F1-Sonderpraesentation-ENG.pdf) · [FIA ruling describing the mechanism](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/decision-document/2020%20Austrian%20Grand%20Prix%20-%20Decision%20-%20Protest%20-%20Car%2044.pdf)

**Mika Häkkinen’s 1998 McLaren-Mercedes MP4-13** would add the era when Mercedes supplied the engine and McLaren built the car. A November 2025 gallery film identifies it in Stuttgart. Its narrower track under the 1998 rules gives visitors a visible change to explore. It merits a fresh label photograph. Hamilton’s **2008 MP4-23**, by contrast, appeared in a six-car entrance-hall presentation with a published end date of **6 January 2025**. That show also featured the W05, W06, W08, W09 and W10. [MP4-13 gallery footage at 6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MShT3tfEHbk&t=360s) · [Mercedes MP4-13 history](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/ddead055-42be-4bef-a636-f4be30241b02) · [Museum special presentation](https://sverige.mercedes-benz-clubs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/89/2024/12/2024-12-16-F1-Sonderpraesentation-ENG.pdf)

**Leave Nico Rosberg’s 2016 W07 off the route for now.** Stuttgart tourism describes his title-winning car on the banking. Zaragoza’s Mobility City also identifies a W07 in its Stuttgart-sourced show, scheduled through **31 January 2027**. The sources do not resolve whether two examples are involved. The Spanish venue lists an original W25 too, making the existing tour’s W25 worth checking on the same visit. ## Three further stops with mechanisms worth seeing

The **1994 NECAR 1** is the strongest follow-up for propulsion. Its fuel-cell installation filled the cargo compartment of an MB 100 van. Fade the body, increase the motor demand and follow hydrogen and air into the stack, electric current to the motor and water out. Mercedes identifies its archive photograph as taken in the Stuttgart museum, and a February 2025 visitor account photographs the van there. The numbered room still needs confirmation. [Mercedes archive and engineering account](https://media.mercedes-benz.pl/30-lat-temu-mercedes-benz-rozpocza-badania-nad-zastosowaniem-ogniw-paliwowych-w-motoryzacji/) · [2025 museum photographs](https://motorillustrated.com/mercedes-benz-museum-in-germany-4-electrified-models-not-to-be-missed/148994/)

The **1938 W 125 twelve-cylinder record car** adds a surprising cooling experiment. Ice and water around the radiator let engineers dispense with a conventional cooling-air intake for a short record run. A visitor could extend the run and watch the ice supply run out. Mercedes identifies the original in Legend 7 in 2018. For a record car with a dedicated museum listing today, the **1939 T 80** is another option. The permanent exhibit comprises the body, subframe and wheels. Its planned record run never took place, so the W 125 supplies the better story about measured performance. [W 125 gallery placement](https://media.mercedes-benz.it/mercedes-benz-w-125-inesauribile-fonte-dispirazione/) · [Mercedes history of both cars](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/en/article/f9a48175-8964-490e-9909-9f35cbea8a6f) · [T 80 museum exhibit](https://www.mercedes-benz.com/en/art-and-culture/museum/exhibition/t-80-record-giant-from-1939/)

The **1960 Mercedes-Benz 300 measuring car** could enrich a stop already in the tour. In 2022 the museum described the one-off wagon in Legend 5 with a cable linking it to the 220 S ahead. In road tests that cable carried measurements back to engineers and their recording equipment. An interaction could send the lead car over a bump, follow an illustrative sensor signal along the cable and replay the trace. A fresh photo of the pair would also resolve the tour’s open question about the 220 S’s gallery. [Museum account of the paired display](https://mb-france.mercedes-benz-clubs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/02/2022Messwagentxt.pdf)

## From shortlist to walking route

The most valuable visit would start at the Legend 7 Pit Stop and banking. A photograph of each car with its adjacent label would settle the W11 and McLaren questions and establish where a visitor should stand. In Legend 6, a photograph of the electric SLS would give the new stop its own reference image. If a dated temporary stop fits the tour, the museum also schedules the **eSprinter and eActros 600 in Collection 5 through 4 April 2027**. That end date belongs on any visitor-facing entry.
